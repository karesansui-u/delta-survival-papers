2. Non-Claims
-------------

These records do not claim:

- external SRE support;
- real-domain typicality;
- M-only sufficiency;
- recovery theorem or survival theorem;
- that synthetic endpoints are real operational mechanisms;
- empirical parity with the L-side Mixed-CSP / q-coloring outside-rerun anchors.


3. Why They Are Kept
--------------------

The value of these benchmarks is anti-collapse discipline:

```text
raw resource count / simple support sum != qualified support
```

They complement the L-side anti-collapse discipline:

```text
raw constraint count != structural loss
```

The current status is therefore:

```text
formal M stack: strong typed-accounting closure
finite M benchmarks: computationally rerunnable support signals
external real-domain M validation: not yet licensed
```
