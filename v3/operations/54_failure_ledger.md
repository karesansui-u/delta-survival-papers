Failure Ledger Policy
=====================

Failures are preserved.

A failed frozen test is not erased, renamed into support, or tuned on the same archive.
It is recorded as one of:

- no-support;
- weak-axis failure;
- mapping failure;
- leakage risk;
- insufficient observability;
- silence.

A later redesign may be tested, but it must receive a new frozen manifest and a new
evidence record.
